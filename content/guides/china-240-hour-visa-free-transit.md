China’s **240-hour visa-free transit** can allow a Shenzhen stop of up to ten days. It is a transit arrangement, so your passport, route and entry port all matter. A Hong Kong–Shenzhen–Hong Kong day trip does not meet the third-country-or-region condition.

**Policy and port list checked on 11 October 2026.** Start with the [National Immigration Administration’s current policy and annex](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147463/c183412/content.html), then confirm your itinerary with your carrier before booking.

## First: Do You Need Transit Permission?

If your passport and visit purpose qualify for a separate visa exemption, that may fit your trip better. Check the [official unilateral visa-free country list](https://www.nia.gov.cn/n794014/n1050181/n1050484/c1731154/content.html). US ordinary passports are not on that 30-day list; the US is included in the transit scheme’s eligible nationalities. These are different entry routes.

## The Four Conditions to Check

1. **Eligible nationality.** The current transit scheme covers ordinary-passport holders from **57 countries**, including the United States. Use the official list rather than assuming that every foreign passport qualifies.
2. **A genuine onward journey.** Have a confirmed departure date and seat to a **third country or region**, within the allowed period. Book it before arrival; an intention to buy a ticket later is insufficient.
3. **An approved entry port and permitted itinerary.** The current scheme lists **65 entry ports** and permitted stay areas in **24 provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities**. Entry and exit rules are not identical.
4. **Valid documents.** The NIA’s [application conditions](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147468/c187308/content.html) require a travel document with at least **three months’ validity** and eligibility to enter your onward destination. Check that destination’s and your carrier’s requirements too.

## Which Entry Ports Work for Shenzhen?

The [NIA’s current port annex](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147463/c183412/part/194194.png) lists these relevant entry points:

| Your route | Eligible entry point to check |
|---|---|
| Flying directly to Shenzhen | **Shenzhen Bao’an International Airport (SZX)** |
| Arriving by an eligible ferry service | **Shekou Port (Passenger)** |
| Taking high-speed rail from Hong Kong | **West Kowloon Station Port of the Guangzhou–Shenzhen–Hong Kong Express Rail Link** |

**Luohu, Futian, Huanggang, Shenzhen Bay, Wenjindu and Sha Tau Kok are not listed as entry ports for this scheme.** Do not use a list of ordinary border crossings as a list of 240-hour transit entry points.

For Guangdong, the annex allows **exit through all open ports across the province**. An exit option does not automatically become an eligible entry option. The [Shenzhen Port Office FAQ](https://ka.sz.gov.cn/szsrmzfkabgswzgkml/szsrmzfkabgswzgkml/ywzsk/tgzy/content/post_12154978.html) also distinguishes entry ports from exit ports.

Choose your entry scheme first, then use the [Hong Kong–Shenzhen crossing guide](/guides/hong-kong-to-shenzhen) to plan transport. The [new Huanggang opening](/guides/new-huanggang-port) does not itself make Huanggang eligible for 240-hour transit entry.

## Does Your Route Count as Transit?

These examples illustrate the third-country-or-region rule; they do not guarantee admission:

| Example | Route condition |
|---|---|
| United States → Shenzhen → Japan | Different arrival and onward countries; other conditions still apply |
| Hong Kong → Shenzhen → Japan | Different arrival and onward regions; use an eligible entry port |
| Hong Kong → Shenzhen → Hong Kong | Return to the same region; does not meet this transit condition |

Include every mainland China connection when checking your route. Ask your carrier and the immigration service about a complicated itinerary before paying for it.

## Where Can You Travel During the Stay?

Cross-province travel is allowed **within the designated stay areas**. This does not mean unrestricted travel everywhere in all 24 provinces: some entries in the annex cover only specified cities or areas. Guangdong is covered province-wide; check the annex for each additional destination.

The NIA permits tourism, business visits and family visits under this scheme. Work, study and news reporting require the relevant permission and visa.

## At Immigration: Documents and Timing

Have your passport, confirmed onward ticket and accommodation details ready. Follow the port’s signs for temporary entry and visa-free transit, complete the required arrival information and apply to the immigration officer.

The [NIA’s application conditions](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147468/c187308/content.html) count the permitted stay from **00:00 on the day after entry**. Use the expiry issued on your temporary entry permit, and leave time to reach your departure port.

Admission is decided by immigration. Do not treat a booked hotel or an event invitation as approval of your transit application.
